How it works

Genome

Eleven genes between 0 and 1, each mapped to a trait: body size, speed, sensing range, three pulls (to grass, prey scent and predator scent), choice randomness, investment per child, breeding threshold, mutation size, and a neutral lineage marker.

World

A wrap-around landscape of grass that regrows towards a capacity set by soil fertility, optionally with seasons. Animals leave scent that spreads and fades.

Behaviour

Each step an animal scores its own spot and eight directions by its pulls, then picks one by a softmax. Low randomness means "go to the best", as in the 1999 code; high randomness approaches the roulette wheel its comments describe.

Life

  • Upkeep scales with size0.75, movement with size × speed², sensing with range.
  • Prey graze; predators catch nearby prey with odds set by relative speed and size, then digest for a while.
  • Above its breeding threshold an animal gives a share of its energy to a child: cloned and mutated, or crossed with a nearby mate using the 1999 blend operator.
  • Animals die of starvation, old age, or being eaten.
